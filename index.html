My dearest Babe,

Happy birthday, Noey. ♡

Sometimes I think about how we met, and I still find it funny how something that seemed so random became one of the most meaningful parts of my life. We met in Roblox, of all places. I remember being curious about you because you looked so cute while we were playing. And you really didn't kill me in that game? Hahaha. I thought that was adorable.

Eventually, I decided to talk to you. It wasn't easy at first because we spoke different languages, and sometimes I didn't know how to express what I wanted to say. Even asking for your Instagram username felt like a whole mission because Roblox kept censoring my messages. Hahaha. But somehow, we kept trying, and I'm really glad we did.

Then we became friends on Instagram, and time started flying. Before I knew it, we were talking every single day, almost nonstop, for nearly a year. Who would've thought that two people who started out as strangers and friends would eventually become something more?

After everything, we finally decided to be together. I know it took me quite a while to tell you how I truly felt. I'm sorry for making you wait. I was shy, and honestly, a little unsure of myself. But I'm glad I found the courage to tell you, because that moment brought us here.

Thank you for every conversation, every laugh, every little moment, and every memory we've made so far. Even the simplest things feel special because they're shared with you. I'm grateful that our paths crossed in the most unexpected way.

On your birthday, I wish you happiness, good health, peace, and the courage to chase everything you dream of. I hope life treats you gently, and I hope you never forget how precious you are.

More than anything, I hope we keep choosing each other, keep learning, and keep growing together. I hope that one day, we finally get to meet, turn all these messages and memories into real moments, and make even more beautiful memories side by side.

I don't know exactly what the future holds, but I know that I want to keep making room for you in mine. Here's to us, to our little story that began in a game, and to all the chapters we haven't written yet.

Happy birthday, my Babe.
With all my love,
Randy ♡
